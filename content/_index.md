I am an Associate Professor in Artificial Intelligence, focusing on AI
and Ethics: how to design and use systems that take ethically-aligned
decisions with our moral values.

I currently work at [CPE Lyon](https://www.cpe.fr/en/), where I teach
data science (storage, analysis) and AI (machine learning, reinforcement
learning, ethical aspects of AI -- bias, fairness, etc.).

I am also part of the [LIRIS](https://liris.cnrs.fr/en/) lab (CNRS-UMR5205),
in the [Cognitive Systems and Multi-Agent Systems 
(SyCoSMA)](https://liris.cnrs.fr/equipe/sycosma) team, in which my research
is at the intersection of hybrid neural-symbolic learning systems,
reinforcement learning, explainability, and machine ethics.
